## How To Use
### How To Add Initiative And Sort
When you enter the page you will see a text field with a placeholder text "Add initiative":
1. Type the initiative roll **after all modifiers are applied** in the text field
2. Click the **plus button** or press Enter on your keyboard.
3. An **initiative card** will appear
4. When you added at least two cards, you can sort them by clicking on the **calc button** (button with two arrows)
### How To Remove Initiative Cards
There are two ways to remove **initiative cards**:

1. Each card has a **remove button**, by clicking it the button is removed
2. Left from the **initiative text field**, there is a **restart button**, by clicking it all the cards are removed
### How To Use Lite Mode

1. To switch to "Lite Mode" just press the "rabbit" button. Notice that after pressing it, the button's icon changes to the one resembling a bird
2. To switch back to "Default Mode" press the "bird" button, after pressing it, the initial rabbit logo is present again
